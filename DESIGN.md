## Brand & Style

This design system blends enterprise technical authority with the expressive, high-craft tactility of modern creative-economy tools. It targets curious professionals, students, and engineers looking for career-defining upskilling in AI, software development, cloud computing, and design.

The emotional tone balances delight and rigor:
- **Playful Craft:** Tactile pill-shapes, warm pastel blocks, organic flower and sparkle glyphs, and a friendly mascot presence (Skillbee) that breaks corporate learning intimidation.
- **Authoritative Rigor:** Crisp high-contrast geometric sans-serif typography, structured grid alignments, solid black interactive accents, and explicit content hierarchies.
- **Physicality:** Generous rounded containers that feel like smooth physical stationery tiles, paired with high-contrast pill actions and spring-like responsiveness.

The visual style is **Playful Modern Neo-Editorial**: structured like a premier editorial journal, energized by candy-coated pastels, anchored with deep carbon ink typography and hairline boundary definitions.

## Colors

The palette uses warm ivory as the canvas foundation, deep carbon black for high-contrast legibility, and an intentional array of luminous pastels to categorize domains and maintain visual momentum:

- **Canvas & Neutrals:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF8F5` (Warm Ivory)
  - Card & Surface Elevated: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White)
  - Subtle Surface Alt: `#F5F3EF` (Tonal Linen)
  - Structural Borders: `#E4E0D8` (Soft Parchment Border) and `#18181B` (Bold Charcoal Border for emphasis)
  - Text Primary: `#18181B` (Deep Carbon Ink)
  - Text Muted: `#71717A` (Stone Grey)

- **Domain Accent Blocks:**
  - **Honey Bee Yellow:** `#FDE047` (Core brand highlight, energetic modules, workshop tags) with `#FEF08A` as soft fill.
  - **Electric Lavender:** `#DDD6FE` (AI, intelligence tracks, community badges) with `#E0E7FF` as soft fill.
  - **Mint Spring:** `#A7F3D0` (Growth, active pathways, project completions) with `#BBF7D0` as tint.
  - **Warm Coral/Blush:** `#FECDD3` (Events, summits, collaborative sprints).
  - **Sky Blue:** `#BAE6FD` (Cloud, technical infrastructure, verified credentials).

Contrasting text on pastels must always default to `#18181B` for readability and graphic punch.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Space Grotesk** for display, headlines, and metrics with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and UI navigation.

- **Display & Headlines:** Space Grotesk provides a modern, slightly technical neo-grotesque edge with distinctive ink-traps that give large text architectural personality.
- **Body & Controls:** Plus Jakarta Sans lends a warm, human, ultra-readable rhythm with rounded terminals that soften technical concepts.
- **Embedded Glyphs:** Headlines often feature inline rounded icon badges (such as geometric blooms, rounded stars, and crescent pills) set inline with the text at matching cap-height to disrupt traditional reading patterns and signal tactile playfulness.
- **Category Superheads:** Use `label-caps` in full uppercase with tracking (+0.08em) as domain anchors preceding card titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a high-density, bento-inspired modular grid system:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid grid up to a 1440px max-width container, 2.5rem outer margins, and 1.25rem gutters. Content is orchestrated into multi-span bento blocks (e.g., Hero visual spanning 5 columns, copy spanning 7 columns; course cards spanning 3 or 4 columns).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Breakpoint at 1024px. Outer margin shifts to 1.5rem, collapsing 4-column module rows into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Breakpoint at 640px. Outer margins compress to 1rem. Bento cards stretch full-width or arrange in horizontal swipeable carousels for chip tags.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (1.5rem) on desktop and `space-md` (1rem) on compact cards. Dense tag groups use `space-xs` (0.25rem) to `space-sm` (0.5rem) gaps to ensure cohesive clustering.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy photographic drop shadows; instead, it uses crisp tactile surface blocks, thin hairline borders, and subtle physical offsets:

- **Surface Contrast:** Depth is established primarily through tinted pastel backgrounds (`#FDE047`, `#DDD6FE`, `#A7F3D0`, `#FECDD3`) resting against the neutral `#FAF8F5` canvas or encapsulated within crisp `#FFFFFF` shells.
- **Subtle Hairlines:** Cards carry a 1px border of `#E4E0D8` (for light cards) or `#18181B` (for high-emphasis tiles).
- **Tactile Hover Offsets:** On interactive cards and primary pill buttons, depth is reinforced on interaction using a quick physical spring transition (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by a tight, diffused contact shadow: `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -6px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`.
- **Badge & Metric Stacks:** Floating stat overlays on 3D visuals utilize backdrop-filter blur (`blur(12px)`) combined with semi-opaque white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`) and a delicate 1px perimeter border.

## Shapes

The shape signature is organic, smooth, and friendly:

- **Base Cards:** Set to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for standard modular content cards and `rounded-2xl` (2rem / 32px) for large hero feature blocks.
- **Controls & Navigation:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for buttons, search bars, and topic chips.
- **Decorative Geometric Glyphs:** Distinctive illustrative cutouts (octagonal starbursts, four-petal clovers, concentric rings, and soft scalloped badges) are deployed inside text lines and card corners to mirror playful educational badges.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Button:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), deep carbon black background (`#18181B`), crisp white text (`#FFFFFF`), with horizontal padding `1.5rem` and vertical padding `0.75rem`. On hover, scale slightly (1.02) with a smooth spring timing curve.
- **Icon Action Button (Circular Arrow):** A circular button (`44px x 44px`), solid `#18181B` fill with a white centered right-angled arrow (`#FFFFFF`), or inverted on dark cards (`#FFFFFF` fill with `#18181B` arrow). Used in bottom corners of course/event tiles.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#18181B` or `#E4E0D8`, `#18181B` typography, pill-shaped.

### Search & Discovery Bar
- Large pill container (`rounded-full`), pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`), subtle 1px border (`#E4E0D8`), containing a magnifying glass icon on the left, `body-md` placeholder text in `#71717A`, and a dedicated black circular submit button on the right.

### Skill Chips & Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.5rem 1rem`.
- Colored by topic cluster:
  - AI / ML: Lavender background (`#DDD6FE`), text `#18181B`
  - Web Dev & Design: Soft Coral (`#FECDD3`), text `#18181B`
  - Data / Business: Warm Honey (`#FDE047`), text `#18181B`
  - Cloud / DevOps: Sky Blue (`#BAE6FD`), text `#18181B`
  - Security / Sustainability: Mint Green (`#A7F3D0`), text `#18181B`

### Bento Cards & Course Tiles
- **Pastel Banner Cards:** Full solid fill in primary pastel (e.g., Honey Yellow `#FDE047`), containing a category uppercase label (`label-caps`), prominent Space Grotesk headline, metadata subtext, a secondary watermark shape (such as a low-opacity floral or starburst silhouette in the background), and the signature circular arrow CTA anchored at the bottom-right.
- **Metric Cards:** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F3EF` background, featuring bold metric figures (`stat-counter`), paired with stacked avatar groups or high-contrast icons (e.g., stars, trending arrows).
- **Dark Inverted Tile:** Solid `#18181B` card with pure white typography, subtle watermark geometry, and a white circle action button for high-contrast visual balance.

### Avatar Stacks
- Overlapping circular portraits (`36px x 36px`), overlapping by `-8px`, each with a 2px `#FFFFFF` outline ring. Trailing count indicator is a dark pill or circle (`#18181B`) with white bold text.
